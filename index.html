Play along with a song

Drop in a song. I'll find its tempo, key and feel, play it, and snap your drawing to the same beat and notes.

Links from SoundCloud, YouTube, Spotify etc. can't be read directly (no API key changes that: those sites don't give other websites the audio). Use the tab option below instead.

  1. Open the song in another tab and press play.
  2. Click the button above, choose that tab, and tick Also share tab audio.
  3. I listen for ~20 s, find the tempo + key, and lock your drawing to it. The song keeps playing in its own tab.

Desktop Chrome / Edge only.
